# FindMate Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

FindMate is an open collaboration space for people with different identities,
backgrounds, abilities, languages, roles, and levels of experience. We commit
to making participation safe, respectful, and useful for everyone acting in
good faith.

The protocol handles unusually sensitive boundaries around owner evidence and
human introductions. Respect for consent, privacy, and a clear request to stop
is therefore part of respectful community behavior, not merely a technical
requirement.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and honest.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Give and receive constructive feedback without pressure or retaliation.
- Respect different experiences, communication styles, and informed choices.
- Protect private information and use fabricated data in public reports.
- Correct mistakes, repair harm where possible, and honor moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or unwanted personal attention.
- Sexualized, insulting, demeaning, discriminatory, or deliberately disruptive
  language or conduct.
- Stereotyping a person from identity, sensitive traits, or an AI-generated
  assessment.
- Publishing, soliciting, or threatening to publish another person's private
  information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring an owner to install, be assessed, publish, star, contact someone,
  exchange identities, or accept an introduction.
- Retaliating against a reporter or attempting to bypass a moderation action.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project-managed social spaces, and public situations where someone is
officially representing FindMate.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an urgent safety or conduct report, use a
[private GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/merc1305/findMate/security/advisories/new).
Do not include owner artifacts, credentials, or unrelated personal details;
share only what is necessary to evaluate the report.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect confidentiality where
practical, and choose a proportionate response. Responses can include a private
clarification, warning, content removal, temporary participation restriction,
or permanent exclusion. Maintainers may reject or remove contributions that
violate this policy and will avoid public discussion of private reports.
